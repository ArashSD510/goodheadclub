The problem

Your head is out in the world all day.

No hair means nothing between your scalp and the sun, the gym, the subway, or the office lights. Most of us just deal with it using a paper towel, a sleeve, or a splash at the bathroom sink.

01

Shine

That end-of-day gleam always shows up right before the meeting or the photo.

02

Sweat

After a workout, a hot commute, or a walk in the sun, it has nowhere to hide.

03

Sunscreen & oil

You're doing the right thing with SPF, and by afternoon your head feels greasy anyway.

04

No sink nearby

Freshening up on the go shouldn't mean finding a bathroom and a roll of paper towels.

How they work

Open. Wipe. Done.

  1. i.

    Pull one out

    Tear the notch, pull a wipe, press the zip shut to keep the rest fresh. Packaging isn't final yet.

  2. ii.

    Front to back

    One easy pass over the scalp, then around the ears and the back of the neck.

  3. iii.

    Get on with your day

    Nothing to rinse. The goal is a clean, matte feel that isn't sticky.
