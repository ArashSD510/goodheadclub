FAQ

Fair questions.

Do I need head wipes if I'm bald?

Probably, if you leave the house. A bald head is skin that's out in the open all day: it gets shiny, sweaty and picks up sunscreen and grime, with no hair to hide any of it. A wipe resets it in seconds, no sink needed.

Why does my bald head smell?

Usually it's sweat and oil that have been sitting there a while. Your scalp is packed with oil and sweat glands, and that build-up (on you, your hats, your pillowcase) can get a little funky. Wipe it off through the day and you're left with a light cedarwood, vetiver and petitgrain scent instead.

Why is my bald head so oily?

Your scalp makes more oil than most of your skin, and with no hair to soak it up, it shows as shine. The textured wipe pulls it off the surface, so less bowling ball, more you.

Can I use head wipes on my face?

They're made for the scalp, so that's where we'd keep them.

Are head wipes flushable?

No. Trash only, please.

Do I need to be fully bald to use these?

Nope. Buzzed short counts. If your scalp sees daylight, you're in the club.
